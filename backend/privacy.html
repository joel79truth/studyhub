Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 5, 2026

StudyHub LUANAR ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, and protection for our mobile and web applications.

1. Information We Collect

A. Account & Authentication Information

When you log in using Google Sign-In, we receive verified identity information:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Profile Picture URL
  • Google User Identifier

B. Academic & Study Data

To provide personalized study and revision capabilities, we store:

  • Program, department, course, and semester selections
  • Quiz attempts, practice scores, and weak-topic mastery records
  • Saved study notes, bookmarks, and past paper downloads

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely to:

  • Authenticate and secure your account session.
  • Track quiz performance and display your study progress.
  • Sync your offline answers and downloads seamlessly when reconnected to the internet.
  • Provide academic assistance and explanations via StudyBot.

We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your personal data with third-party advertisers.

3. Offline Storage

StudyHub LUANAR is built with an offline-first architecture. Downloaded notes, slides, and cached quiz questions are saved locally on your device via secure IndexedDB storage. You can delete offline cached files at any time through the app settings or Android device storage settings.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app connects with trusted services to deliver functionality:

  • Google Identity & Play Services: For secure single sign-on.
  • Supabase: For cloud database management and session authentication.
  • Firebase: For cloud notifications and crash analytics.
  • Groq / OpenAI: For academic AI tutor assistance.

5. Account & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You may request account deletion or removal of your study history at any time by contacting us at joel79truth@gmail.com.
