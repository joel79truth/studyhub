# Privacy Policy for StudyHub LUANAR

**Last Updated: September 5, 2026**

StudyHub LUANAR ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile and web application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Account & Authentication Information
When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive basic profile information authorized by you through Google:
- Full Name
- Email Address
- Profile Picture URL
- Google Account Identifier

### B. Study and Academic Data
To provide personalized study and revision features, we store:
- Program, department, course, and semester selections
- Quiz attempts, scores, and mastery history
- Saved notes, bookmarks, and past paper downloads

### C. Technical and Device Data
When using our services online, standard technical logs may include:
- Device platform (Android / Web)
- Network connectivity status (to enable offline synchronization)
- Crash logs and performance diagnostics (via Firebase)

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:
- Authenticate and maintain your user session.
- Track and display your quiz scores, revision streaks, and topic mastery.
- Synchronize notes and quiz submissions when transitioning between offline and online modes.
- Provide contextual AI tutoring via StudyBot academic assistance.
- Notify you of newly uploaded past papers or course notes (if notifications are enabled).

**We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.**

---

## 3. Offline Storage & On-Device Data

StudyHub LUANAR is built with an offline-first architecture. 
- Downloaded lecture notes, past exam papers, and quiz state are stored locally on your device using IndexedDB / local storage.
- You can clear this offline data at any time through the app settings or by clearing the app's cache in Android system settings.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

We integrate with trusted third-party service providers to power specific app features:
- **Google Play Services & Google Identity:** For secure OAuth authentication.
- **Firebase / Google Cloud:** For push notifications and cloud infrastructure.
- **Supabase:** For secure database storage and session token verification.
- **Groq / OpenAI:** For processing AI academic tutor queries.

These providers process data strictly in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your account and academic data for as long as your account remains active. 

If you wish to delete your account, wipe your quiz history, or remove your data from our servers, you may submit a deletion request by contacting us at:
**support@studyhub-luanar.com** (or via the developer contact provided below).

---

## 6. Children’s Privacy

Our services are designed for university students and adult learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted with an updated "Last Updated" date.
